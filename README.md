# Supermart Grocery Sales Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Supermart Grocery Sales Dashboard** is an Excel-based data analytics project created to analyze grocery sales performance, profitability, product categories, discounts, and geographic performance.

The dashboard uses **Excel formulas, PivotTables, KPI cards, and charts** to convert raw grocery sales data into meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Objectives

### Objective 1 — Overall Sales Performance
Analyze the overall performance of the grocery business.

- Calculate Total Sales
- Calculate Total Profit
- Calculate Total Orders
- Calculate Profit Margin
- Analyze sales by category
- Analyze sales trends over time
- Compare sales across regions

### Objective 2 — Product Performance
Understand which categories and sub-categories are driving sales and profit.

- Analyze sales by category
- Analyze sales by sub-category
- Compare sub-category sales and profit
- Calculate profit margin by sub-category
- Identify high-sales sub-categories with comparatively lower profitability

> **Note:** The dataset does not contain a Product Name column. Therefore, sub-category level analysis is used for product-performance analysis.

### Objective 3 — Profit and Discount
Understand profitability and the relationship between discount and profit.

- Analyze profit by category
- Analyze profit by region
- Compare discount and profit
- Analyze the relationship between discount and profit using a scatter chart

### Objective 4 — Geographic Performance
Understand which locations contribute most to the business.

- Analyze sales by state
- Identify top cities by sales
- Compare city-wise sales and profit
- Analyze profit margin by city
- Identify locations with high sales but comparatively lower profitability
